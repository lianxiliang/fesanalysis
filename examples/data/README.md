The downloadable archive includes sample.xyz.gz: every twentieth frame from the
user-provided 2,401-frame, 360-atom output(1).xyz. Original timestep comments are
preserved. This gives 121 frames for the geometry notebook.

The coordinates have NO cell or explicit time unit. The example supplies the
study's 10.39 x 10.18 x 36.17 A cell. Confirm that this is your simulated cell.
The MD timestep must be provided before matching COLVAR.

These coordinates are user-provided research data, not licensed under the code's
MIT license. They are git-ignored. Confirm permission before publishing them.
No bias file is paired with this sample: the earlier COLVAR0 did not match its
recomputed CVs beyond frame zero under either tested timestep (0.5 or 1 fs).
Consequently the sample demonstrates geometry, NOT an unbiased physical FES.
